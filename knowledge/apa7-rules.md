# APA 7 rules the office applies

Sources: Purdue OWL, *Reference List: Basic Rules* (APA 7); Bangkok University,
*รูปแบบการเขียนอ้างอิงและบรรณานุกรม (APA 7th)*, updated 13 Mar 2025. A
customer's institution guide overrides anything here; record the difference in
the job notes and, if it recurs, in [thai-rules.md](thai-rules.md).

## Reference list page

- Heading **References** (Thai: **บรรณานุกรม** or the institution's word), bold, centred, new page.
- Whole list double-spaced.
- Hanging indent 0.5 inch: first line flush left, later lines indented.
- Alphabetical by the first author's surname; entries with no author sort by title.
- Italic: book titles, journal names, and the journal volume number. Article and chapter titles are not italic.

## Authors

- 1–20 authors: list every one, comma-separated, `&` before the last.
- 21 or more: the first 19, then ` . . . `, then the last author.
- Initials with periods and spaces: `Hair, J. F.`
- Group author spelled out in full.

## Entry templates

| Type | Template |
|---|---|
| Book | Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). *Title of book* (Xth ed.). Publisher. |
| Chapter in edited book | Author, A. (Year). Chapter title. In E. Editor (Ed.), *Book title* (pp. x–y). Publisher. |
| Journal article | Author, A. (Year). Article title. *Journal Name, Volume*(Issue), x–y. https://doi.org/... |
| Thesis | Author, A. (Year). *Title* [Master's thesis, University]. Database. URL |
| Conference paper in proceedings | as a chapter: In E. Editor (Ed.), *Proceedings title* (pp. x–y). Publisher. |
| Web page | Author. (Year, Month Day). *Title*. Site Name. URL |

APA 7 changes from APA 6 that customers trip on: no publisher location; up to
20 authors before the ellipsis (APA 6: 7); DOI written as `https://doi.org/...`;
no "Retrieved from" before a URL.

## In-text citations

| Case | Parenthetical | Narrative |
|---|---|---|
| 1 author | (Vygotsky, 1978) | Vygotsky (1978) |
| 2 authors | (Greenberg & Baron, 2003) | Greenberg and Baron (2003) |
| 3+ authors, from the first citation | (Hair et al., 2010) | Hair et al. (2010) |
| Group with abbreviation, first then later | (Australian Institute of Health and Welfare [AIHW], 2005) → (AIHW, 2005) | — |
| Same author, same year | (Han et al., 2021a, 2021b) | — |
| Several works | (Holmberg et al., 2004; Sartre, 1962; Vygotsky, 1978), alphabetical, `;` between | — |
| No date | (Author, n.d.) / (ผู้แต่ง, ม.ป.ป.) | — |

Mixed Thai and foreign authors in one parenthesis: the Bangkok University
guide puts Thai authors first, then foreign, each alphabetical. Treat this as
that institution's rule unless a guide says otherwise.

## Cross-check between text and list

Every in-text citation must have a list entry with the same surname and year,
and every list entry must be cited at least once. Report each mismatch; never
delete an entry or citation on the customer's behalf.
